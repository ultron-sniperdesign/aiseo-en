**Internal linking means connecting the pages of your own site with internal links.** It helps readers, search engines, and AI systems work out what belongs together and which pages carry higher priority within the site. In practice what decides it is descriptive anchor text, whether the page is reachable from relevant places, and whether anything links to it at all.

## What internal links actually do

Three things that are broadly beyond dispute:

- **A path to the content** — crawlers follow links to reach pages. Without an internal link, discovery and placement within the site structure is usually harder, even though a page can surface another way, through the sitemap or an external link.
- **Context** — anchor text says what is on the other side before anyone clicks through.
- **Relationships and priority** — the link structure signals what belongs together and which pages carry higher priority within the site.

<Insight tone="aio">
What you cannot claim: how much "power" a link passes, or exactly how AI systems weight internal links. Nobody publishes those weights. Stick to what can be verified — links help content get discovered and help make the relationships clear. That is reason enough to do them properly.
</Insight>

## What this adds in AI answers

Many AI systems work with retrieved chunks of content when they answer. Descriptive anchor text helps them understand what is on the other side, and internal linking shows that the site devotes more than one isolated page to the topic. The same principle drives pillar content structure, which is covered in [how to structure pillar content](/blog/how-to-structure-pillar-content/).

<Insight tone="warn">
Do not overrate it. Internal links will not rescue content that has nothing to offer — they only make it easier to find what is already there. If a page answers no question at all, ten links will not save it; [how AI cites sources](/blog/how-ai-cites-sources/) explains why.
</Insight>

## Key takeaways

- **Anchor text is context** — descriptive wording instead of here and more.
- **A link from the text usually beats one from the footer** — it carries clearer context.
- **Hunt for orphan and thinly linked pages regularly** — publishing is not enough.
- **Relevance decides it, not volume** — there is no magic number of links.
- **Do not overrate it** — links make value easier to find, they do not create it; content structure is covered in [content for AI](/blog/ai-seo-content/).
