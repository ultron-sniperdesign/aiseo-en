**When AI states something false about your company, you usually can't rewrite the answer directly** — there's no standard interface for editing it yourself. What you can realistically do is fix the sources the system draws on, state the correct information clearly on your own site, and report the error. The effect is indirect, with no guarantee and no firm timeline.

Right up front, it helps to separate two things that get confused easily. **Directly editing the answer itself** usually isn't available in the standard interface. **Reporting the error, though, you can do** — some platforms have feedback controls for it and let you update the related business record or profile. What you don't get to see is how exactly the report is evaluated and when it might take effect.

## Start with the cause

This is the heart of it. "AI is lying about us" isn't one situation, it's three — and each one gets handled differently.

Hallucinations may be more common where clear, consistent material is missing, but they also come out of a bad synthesis of several sources or a case of mistaken entity. So the risk can grow for brands with less consistent public material out there; on why models make things up, see the article on [AI-generated content](/blog/ai-generated-content-visibility/).

## Where this article stops

Two things this piece doesn't address and can't.

**It can't promise a result.** Even after every step is done correctly, there's no guaranteeing the answer will change, or when. Anyone claiming otherwise is selling certainty they don't have.

**It doesn't cover the legal side.** Alongside fixing sources, it can sometimes be worth reporting the output to the platform, correcting the related business record, or making a formal request to the source; the specific route depends on the type of error and the service. If a false claim is doing real harm to you, talking to a lawyer is the right call. Here we're staying with what you can do on the content and technical side.

## Key takeaways

- **You can't rewrite the model, but you can fix the sources** — everything that works aims at where the claim comes from.
- **Cause first, then fix** — a stale fact, a third-party source, and a hallucination each get handled differently.
- **Hallucinations often grow where clear material is missing** — though it can also be a case of mixed-up brands.
- **Consistency across your site and profiles** — contradictions leave the choice to the machines.
- **No guarantee and no timeline** — verify repeatedly, see the [AI visibility test](/blog/ai-visibility-test/), and never promise anyone a date.
