## AI SEO for B2B: where the shortlist gets decided today

**AI SEO for B2B is about optimizing your content and your brand so buyers run into you through AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini) before they ever contact your sales team.** A B2B purchase has a long research phase — the buyer compares vendors (the vendor shortlist), reads reviews, and builds an internal business case. Available surveys suggest that phase increasingly happens inside AI tools too. If AI doesn't mention or cite you on the key research queries, your odds of making the shortlist drop, even with a strong product.

This analysis adds **B2B specifics** to the broader [practical SEO for AI checklist](/blog/seo-for-ai-checklist/), and it's a different animal from [SEO for e-commerce in the AI era](/blog/seo-for-ecommerce-ai-era/), which covers B2C.

<Insight tone="accent" icon="!" label="The takeaway">
  In B2B, the vendor shortlist can take shape while the buyer is working with AI tools — often before your sales team is in the conversation. A citation in AI isn't just a bonus, then; it can raise the odds your brand shows up early in the selection.
</Insight>

## Buyers have moved the journey into AI

Public analyses from 2026 show that most B2B buyers now use AI tools during purchase research:

- B2B surveys from 2025–2026 show a growing share of buyers who treat AI tools as one of their most important information sources when researching vendors — sometimes ahead of vendor websites, product specialists, or sales reps. The exact share varies by methodology, industry, and company size.
- A larger share of B2B buyers now uses AI tools somewhere in the research process — often before they visit a vendor's site or talk to a sales team.
- The specific percentages differ from one analysis to the next, but the direction is consistent: AI's share of B2B research is growing.

Treat the numbers as a trend, not a fixed benchmark — the actual values depend on the industry, the segment, and how the measurement was done.

## How to get your B2B site ready for AI

Same AI SEO fundamentals, with B2B emphases:

- **Test your AI visibility** — run the typical research queries in your category through ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. Watch where you get cited and where you don't.
- **Build comparison content** — X vs. Y, the best solution for a specific use case, alternatives to X. Write it honestly, not as thinly veiled advertising — see [comparison articles for AI](/blog/comparison-articles-for-ai/).
- **Strengthen reputation and reviews** — G2, industry directories, third-party reviews. AI tools can pull from sources beyond your own site when they assemble an answer, which is why reputation and documented mentions help.
- **Structure your product and feature pages** for citation — clear value, pricing, use cases, and integrations, each in its own passage.
- **Measure conversions from AI, not volume** — AI visits in B2B tend to be highly qualified but low in volume; track them in a segment and judge them on conversion value. See [AI traffic and conversions](/blog/ai-traffic-and-conversions/).
