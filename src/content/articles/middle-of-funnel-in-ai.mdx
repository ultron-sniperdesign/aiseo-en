**The middle of the funnel in the AI era is the stage where someone compares options and picks a solution — and that is exactly where the center of gravity of the decision is moving.** Because AI Overviews answer informational queries directly, the top of the funnel loses clicks and value shifts from "what is X" content to "which X is best for me" content — and to whether AI names you at all. This article covers what is happening to the funnel and where the bigger value sits today.

## What AI does to the funnel

The classic funnel had informational queries at the top ("what is X", "how do I do Y"), consideration in the middle, and the decision at the bottom. But AI Overviews answer informational queries directly on the results page — and that is precisely where the clicks disappear fastest. We cover that effect in detail in the article on the [zero-click effect](/blog/zero-click-and-ai-overviews/).

According to marketing analyses from 2026, the middle of the funnel also collapses into a single AI answer more often: discovering a brand, evaluating it, and comparing vendors can happen **at once inside one AI conversation**, not in sequential stages spread over weeks.

<Insight tone="aio">
The funnel did not collapse, it just got rearranged. AI now handles most of the informational top floor itself. Your opportunity moves to where people actually choose — comparison, decision, and brand.
</Insight>

## Why it pays to be named

In the AI era it is not enough to be a source somewhere in the background of an answer — the point is to be **named in it**. Marketing analyses from 2026 suggest that brands cited by name get meaningfully more clicks per impression than brands absent from the answer (the figure quoted is roughly 120% more). That is data from proprietary samples, but the direction is consistent.

How you get named is tied to [how AI cites sources](/blog/how-ai-cites-sources/) and to [building brand mentions](/blog/ai-brand-mentions/) in the places AI draws from.

<Insight tone="warn">
Lower volume does not mean lower value. AI visitors can arrive better informed and closer to a decision, so marketing analyses suggest they can convert at a higher rate than average organic traffic. Smaller but better-qualified traffic can produce a comparable or better result than high volume from the top of the funnel.
</Insight>

## What to take away

- **AI is eating the informational top of the funnel** — it answers "what is X" and "how do I do Y" directly, and the clicks disappear.
- **The middle collapses into a single AI answer** — discovery, consideration, and comparison all happen at once.
- **Value shifts to the decision** — comparisons, selection criteria, "best X for Y".
- **Being named has value** — marketing analyses suggest brands cited by name get substantially more clicks.
- **Quality over volume** — AI visitors are lower volume, but better qualified.
- **Change your measurement too** — track citations and conversions; check where you stand with an [AI visibility test](/blog/ai-visibility-test/).
