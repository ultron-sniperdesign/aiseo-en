## Zero-click in the AI era: what's actually happening

**Zero-click search is when a user gets the answer directly in the results and never clicks a link.** On a lot of queries, AI Overviews can push that share higher — the user sometimes gets a good enough answer right there in the result and has no reason to continue to a site. But for brands this isn't purely lost clicks: some zero-click queries build the brand at the same time, through citations. The key is to **measure the impact and compensate deliberately**, not to panic over a traffic dip.

This analysis adds an **analytical angle** to the [guide on turning off AI Overviews](/blog/how-to-turn-off-ai-overviews/): how to measure zero-click in Google Search Console, when it's a problem, and how to make up for the loss. For broader performance tracking, the [guide to measuring SEO performance](/blog/measuring-seo-performance/) is a good companion.

<Insight tone="accent" icon="!" label="What to take away">
  Not every zero-click is a loss. A citation in AI Overviews can build the brand even when the user doesn't click. The goal isn't to save every zero-click — it's to tell the good ones from the bad ones and compensate for the valuable visits you lose.

## How AI Overviews change CTR and the zero-click share

Zero-click isn't new, but AI Overviews amplify it considerably:

- The share of zero-click queries in Google is rising — various analyses from 2025–2026 show it approaching **two-thirds** of queries in some markets. Treat that as a rough trend, not a universal benchmark.
- On queries that trigger AI Overviews, the zero-click share tends to be significantly higher still.
- Organic CTR on queries with an AI Overview drops noticeably according to those analyses — but the spread is wide and depends on query type, vertical, and what the result looks like.

Specific percentages differ between studies depending on market, device, query type, and measurement methodology — read them as a trend, not a benchmark.

## How to make up for lost clicks

If zero-click is taking over valuable queries, focusing on click volume alone isn't enough. Move both your measurement and your strategy:

- **Build the brand through AI citations** — a citation in AI Overviews can raise brand visibility even without a click, as long as the source or brand is clearly named in the result. [E-E-A-T for AI](/blog/e-e-a-t-for-ai/) helps here.
- **Invest in the middle of the funnel** — comparisons, reviews, branded queries, and queries about vendors, alternatives, and pricing, where the purchase decision gets made.
- **Work on mentions off your own site** — [mentions and reviews](/blog/ai-brand-mentions/) can help strengthen your brand's discoverability and credibility across the ecosystem of sources AI answers draw from.
- **Measure indirect metrics too** — branded search, mentions in AI answers, conversion quality, not just visit volume.
