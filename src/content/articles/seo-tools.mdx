## Why the <span class="hl">2026</span> SEO stack <strong>looks different</strong>

The classic SEO stack didn't fall apart over the last two years — it **grew a new layer**: [measuring AI visibility](/blog/ai-visibility-test/). Alongside the tools for keyword research, backlinks, rank tracking, and [technical audits](/blog/seo-audit-checklist/) (Ahrefs, Semrush, SE Ranking) came platforms that track [citations](/blog/how-ai-cites-sources/) and [brand mentions](/blog/ai-brand-mentions/) in AI answers (Otterly, Profound, Brand Radar). The classic tools still cover most of the work; the AI layer is an add-on for specific use cases, not a replacement.

The second change: **the entry price dropped**. Ahrefs launched a Starter plan at $29/mo, down from a $99 minimum. That moved SEO tools out of the "agency luxury" bracket and into reach for small teams that actually work on SEO. Price is no longer the primary barrier — for most small sites the bottleneck is time, knowledge, and implementation capacity, not $29/mo.

## <span class="hl">Seven categories</span> of <strong>SEO tools in 2026</strong>

Don't build your stack around product names — build it around **functional categories**. Each tool typically dominates one or two categories; **don't buy a second tool where it overlaps with the first**. Extend the stack only where the first tool falls short.

### 1. Your own data (free, mandatory)

Google Search Console is the minimum baseline, and Bing Webmaster Tools makes sense wherever Bing and Copilot carry weight in your market. Not every project needs both with the same intensity, but without your own data you're managing SEO blind. Both are free, with official data straight from the search engines.

### 5. Performance — Core Web Vitals and speed

Core Web Vitals have been page experience signals since 2021. In practice they're typically a **tie-breaker, not a primary ranking factor** — they carry secondary weight next to relevance, content, authority, and intent match. Prioritize them on high-traffic templates, product detail pages, category pages, and anywhere speed genuinely moves conversions. Don't burn time polishing a Lighthouse score if you have bigger SEO problems.

### 6. Structured data and schema

Structured data helps clarify the entity, the content type, product information, and author and organization details. **It is not a guarantee of better rankings or a citation in AI Overviews** — treat it as hygiene for machine readability, not as an acquisition lever in its own right. Google has scaled back FAQPage and HowTo rich results in recent years; their impact depends on your site type and topic.

A new category that emerged in 2024–2025. It tracks how often your brand shows up in answers from AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Google AI Overviews). **AI citation tracking mainly pays off where your audience demonstrably uses AI tools to choose** vendors, products, or expert sources — typically upper-tier B2B, premium brands, and technical products. For local services, small online stores, and sites with no branded demand, it often ranks below technical hygiene, content, and conversion measurement.

## <strong>Recommended stacks</strong> by <span class="hl">situation</span>

Instead of hunting for "the best tool of 2026," look for the stack that fits your situation. Here are four proven configurations:

### Stack 1 — Small site or blog ($29–58/mo)

- **Free baseline:** GSC + PageSpeed Insights + Rich Results Test
- **Paid:** Ahrefs Starter ($29/mo) **or** SE Ranking Essential ($65/mo)
- **Optional AI:** Otterly Lite ($29/mo)

**Good for:** a personal blog, a small online store under 100 products, a local business.

### Stack 2 — SMB e-commerce or B2B SaaS ($150–450/mo depending on the mix)

Structure this by priority, not all at once:

- **P0, non-negotiable:** GSC + GA4/Matomo + conversion tracking + PageSpeed and Rich Results Test (free baseline plus analytics)
- **P1, depending on use case:** one main tool for competitors and rank tracking — Ahrefs Lite/Standard ($129–249/mo) **or** Semrush Pro ($139.95/mo) **or** SE Ranking Essential/Pro ($65–139/mo)
- **P2, only once you scale:** Otterly Pro ($99/mo) — only if AI research genuinely shapes your audience's buying journey and you have the budget for it

Realistic range: **$150–450/mo**, depending on whether you take the Lite or Standard tier of your main tool and whether you add AI tracking. **Good for:** a growing store with 100–1,000 products, B2B SaaS, a content brand.

### Stack 3 — Agency stack ($800–1,500/mo)

- **Core:** Ahrefs Standard ($249) + Semrush Guru ($249.95)
- **Crawl:** Screaming Frog full ($22/mo)
- **AI era:** Otterly Pro or Profound (from $200/mo)
- **Specialized:** Surfer SEO (content), Brand24 (mentions), Sitebulb (client reports)

**Good for:** an agency managing 10+ client sites, or an in-house team at a large company.

### Stack 4 — Enterprise or global brand ($2,000+/mo)

- **Profound** (custom pricing from $500+/mo) for AI tracking
- **Ahrefs Advanced** ($449/mo) + **Semrush Business** ($499.95/mo)
- **Conductor / BrightEdge** (enterprise SEO platforms, $50k+/year)
- A custom data pipeline (BigQuery + Looker or Tableau)

**Good for:** Fortune 500 companies, e-commerce with 100k+ SKUs, media groups.

## <strong>What to do</strong> <span class="hl">this week</span>

If you haven't touched SEO tools yet, one step a week gets you to a working setup in a month:

1. **Week 1:** Set up Google Search Console, verify ownership, submit your sitemap.
2. **Week 2:** Check Core Web Vitals in PageSpeed Insights for your top 10 pages. Validate your schema with the Rich Results Test.
3. **Week 3:** Pick one paid tool (Ahrefs Starter at $29 or SE Ranking Essential at $65). Set it up and import your keywords from GSC.
4. **Week 4:** If you're targeting a B2B or premium brand: set up Otterly Lite ($29/mo). Define the 5–10 key queries you want to be cited for in AI answers.

After a month you have a working measurement and benchmarking layer — a functional baseline for managing SEO, not a complete system; that depends on how you set up tracking and what you decide to measure — for under $100/mo.
