## Bing SEO for AI: why Bing indexing is worth your time

**Bing SEO for AI means getting your site into Bing's index and removing a technical barrier to being found in AI search.** By available accounts, ChatGPT can use Bing as one of its important sources when it searches the web, so if your site is missing from Bing, ChatGPT has a harder time finding it. This guide shows you how to get your site into Bing and how to speed up indexing — through Bing Webmaster Tools and the IndexNow protocol.

Being indexed in Bing is **an important technical layer of discoverability, not a guarantee of a citation** (as of May 2026). It opens the door; the citation itself comes down to the quality and structure of your content, which is what [SEO for ChatGPT](/blog/chatgpt-seo/) covers.

## Why Bing matters for AI search

In classic SEO, you focus on Google. With AI it's different: by available accounts, ChatGPT also uses **Bing's index** when it searches the web. That makes Bing a potentially important channel for ChatGPT — and one most sites never touch.

This doesn't mean abandoning Google. It means **adding Bing** as a second channel that matters for AI.

## Step 1: Verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools

The starting point is getting your site into Bing:

- Add your site in **Bing Webmaster Tools** and verify ownership.
- The fastest route is **importing from Google Search Console** — the site and its sitemaps carry over in a couple of minutes.

## Step 2: Submit your sitemap

An XML sitemap helps Bing discover all of your pages:

- In Bing Webmaster Tools, add the address of your `sitemap.xml`.
- Check that the sitemap returns HTTP 200 and contains current URLs.

## Step 3: Turn on IndexNow

**IndexNow** is an open protocol supported by Bing that lets you **tell it immediately** about new, changed, or deleted content — instead of waiting for a normal crawl:

- The ping goes out within **seconds**. That means Bing can discover the content sooner, though actually getting into the index, and how long that takes, can't be guaranteed.
- On WordPress you enable it through an SEO plugin (Rank Math, for example); plenty of platforms have it built in.

For opening your site up technically to AI crawlers, see the [guide to robots.txt for AI](/blog/robots-txt-for-ai-crawlers/).

## What Bing and IndexNow won't do (and what to watch out for)

Here's what matters:

- **Indexing isn't a citation** — getting into Bing means the page can technically be found, but ChatGPT cites based on relevance, currency, and trustworthiness.
- **IndexNow won't speed up quality** — it gets your content there faster, but it won't make weak content better.
- **Watch your crawlers too** — check that your `robots.txt` isn't needlessly blocking relevant search crawlers, especially Bingbot and, depending on your strategy, OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot.
- **Bing isn't the only channel** — it matters for ChatGPT, but AI visibility has more layers (content, mentions, structured data).
