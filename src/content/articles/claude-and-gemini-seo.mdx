## Claude and Gemini SEO: why they belong on your radar

**Claude and Gemini are two AI platforms that get less SEO attention than ChatGPT or Perplexity — yet they can shape where and how your brand shows up in AI answers.** For web answers, Gemini leans on Google Search, so it benefits from solid classic SEO. Claude, according to the available analyses, weighs reviews and reputation more heavily. The foundation is the same for both, though: crawlable, comprehensible content, structured data, freshness, and credibility.

This guide extends the platform set — [SEO for ChatGPT](/blog/chatgpt-seo/), [optimizing for Perplexity](/blog/perplexity-seo/), and [Google AI Mode](/blog/google-ai-mode-content/) — with two platforms that get discussed far less.

<Insight tone="accent" icon="!" label="The takeaway">
  Claude and Gemini rarely call for two separate strategies. Start from the shared foundation for AI search, then tune the emphasis — classic SEO and authority for Gemini, reputation and reviews for Claude.
</Insight>

## Gemini: it leans on Google Search

For web-grounded answers, Gemini leans on Google Search — it uses web results as the basis for its answer (grounding). For visibility, that means:

- The foundation is being **discoverable and well rated** in classic search.
- Authoritative and official sources typically have **a better shot at a citation** in Gemini.
- Strong classic SEO and content prepared for AI Overviews are a good starting point for Gemini too — that doesn't mean Gemini will use the same sources or the same order as classic results.

## Claude: it tracks reputation and reviews more closely

With Claude, external citation analyses often point to **reputation, reviews, and mentions** off your own site. It isn't an officially confirmed ranking mechanism, but strengthening those signals makes practical sense:

- Alongside the general basics, work on **building trust and reputation** beyond your own site.
- [Third-party mentions and reviews](/blog/ai-brand-mentions/) help.
- Credibility in general is covered in [E-E-A-T for AI](/blog/e-e-a-t-for-ai/).

## Crawlers and robots.txt: what to watch for

With both Claude and Gemini, it's worth knowing who reads your content and how:

<Insight tone="seo" icon="i" label="Google-Extended is not a rankings switch">
  Google-Extended is not a switch for Search rankings. Per Google, it controls how your content is used for selected generative products such as Gemini Apps and the Vertex AI generative APIs. Indexing and rankings in classic Search are handled by Googlebot.
</Insight>

Anthropic's documentation lists three crawlers — **ClaudeBot** (training crawl), **Claude-User** (fetches a page at a user's request), and **Claude-SearchBot** (Claude's web search). Manage their access according to Anthropic's current `robots.txt` rules. Controlling AI crawlers in detail is covered in the [robots.txt guide for AI](/blog/robots-txt-for-ai-crawlers/).
