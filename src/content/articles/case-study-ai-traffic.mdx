**This is part one of a living case study.** Our own store — the car-care e-commerce site Sniper Design runs — gets recommended in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI tools, even though we're only now starting deliberate AI SEO work on it. We're publishing the baseline numbers **before optimization**, and in a few months we'll put the after picture next to them. No borrowed study — our own GA4 and Search Console data.

## How much AI tools actually send

Over twelve months (July 17, 2025 – July 16, 2026), AI tools brought in **1,867 visits** out of a total of 394,000 — roughly **0.5 percent**. Nine out of ten AI visits came from ChatGPT (1,688), the rest from Perplexity (126), Gemini (28), Copilot (23), and a handful from Claude and Grok.

Through March the volume held around 110–160 visits a month. **In April and May 2026 it jumped to 223–241 — a rise of more than half** over the running average. We're not reading anything into the June dip yet; whether it's seasonality or noise, the next few months will tell.

Half a percent sounds like a rounding error. But the volume matters less than **who those visitors are** — and that's where the data turns.

## Google AI Overviews: 200,000 impressions in two months

Referrals are only the visible part, though. In 2026, Search Console added a new beta report — **Generative AI features** — showing how often a site appears directly inside Google's AI answers (AI Overviews and friends). For our store: **200,000 impressions between May 18 and July 15, 2026**, averaging around **3,400 impressions a day**.

<Insight tone="warning">
The referral numbers in this study are a **floor estimate of AI's influence**. Those 200,000 AI Overviews impressions don't produce a visit, but they build awareness — and clicks from AI Overviews can't be separated from regular organic in Search Console anyway. A large share of AI influence is "dark traffic": someone gets a recommendation in ChatGPT or in a Google AI answer, then searches the brand or types the address directly, and it lands in analytics as organic or direct. The pattern backs that up: the strongest query in Search Console is the brand name itself, and direct traffic accounts for more than a fifth of the site.
</Insight>

## Key takeaways

- **AI referrals are a fraction of a percent of traffic today** — anyone promising "20 percent of your traffic from ChatGPT within six months" isn't showing you data.
- **The quality, though, is exceptional**: ~4× the conversion rate and ~3× the revenue per visit compared with Google. AI sends customers who've already decided.
- **Visibility in AI is an order of magnitude bigger than referrals**: 200,000 AI Overviews impressions in two months — a layer you won't see in GA4.
- **The trend points up** — April and May 2026 brought a jump of more than half.
- **What gets cited is content, not the catalog**: articles, courses, physical locations. An online store with no educational content has nothing to cite.
- **Measurable referrals are a floor estimate** — part of AI's influence hides in brand search, direct traffic, and AI answers that never get clicked.
