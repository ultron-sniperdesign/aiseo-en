**FAQ rich results were the expandable questions and answers that appeared right in Google Search — and Google ended them on May 7, 2026.** FAQPage markup remains valid, though, and per Google's public documentation it is still processed to understand the page; structured questions and answers can also be useful to AI systems. What ended is the display feature, not the value of structured data. This article covers what exactly happened, why the markup is worth keeping in most cases, and when to remove it instead.

## What exactly happened

FAQ rich results were a type of rich result: expandable questions and answers from a page's FAQ section, shown under the link in Search. Per Google's public Search Central documentation, that feature is being retired — and it is happening in three phases.

The worst option is not keeping the markup, and it is not deleting it — it is leaving it out of sync with the visible content. If you take an FAQ section off a page, take its FAQPage markup off too. Consistency between structured data and what the user actually sees matters more now than it used to.

## It fits a broader shift in structured data

The end of FAQ rich results is not an isolated event. HowTo lost its rich result earlier, and Google keeps repeating that AI Overviews and AI Mode need [no special structured data](/blog/aeo-geo-is-still-seo/). The direction is consistent: structured data is meant to help systems understand content, not to decorate the result.

That does not mean structured data is losing its point. Quite the opposite — when it accurately describes visible content, it helps Google and AI alike. Which types to deploy and how is covered in the article on [structured data for AI](/blog/structured-data-for-ai/).

## What to take away

- **Google ended FAQ rich results on May 7, 2026** — the expandable questions are gone from Search results.
- **FAQPage markup remains valid** — per Google, both Google and AI systems still read it.
- **What ended is the display feature, not the value** — the point was never the structured data, it was the content.
- **Keep the markup when it matches** the visible questions and answers; remove only thin or mismatched FAQs.
- **The retirement runs in three phases** — rich results (May), the GSC report (June), and API support (August 2026, still to come).
- **The priority is shifting** from decorating search results toward clear structure and citability in AI.
