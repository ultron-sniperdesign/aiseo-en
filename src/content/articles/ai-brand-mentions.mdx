## Brand mentions: why AI reaches for other sites

**Brand mentions are occurrences of a company, product, or site name on third-party pages — in reviews, communities, comparison sites, or industry media.** They can matter for AI answers because they help verify that a brand is described consistently beyond its own site. According to published analyses from 2025–2026, citations in AI answers often point to third parties, not just to brand domains.

This is the **off-page layer** of AI visibility. How to write your own content so it can be cited is covered in [AI SEO content](/blog/ai-seo-content/); this piece is about what gets written about you **elsewhere**. Your own site remains the foundation — off-page adds independent confirmation on top of it.

## Why AI often cites third-party sources too

The logic is simple: a claim you make about yourself on your own site has no independent confirmation. But when several independent sources describe a brand consistently, AI can read that as a **trust signal** — agreement across sources.

- A brand that exists only on its own site has no outside confirmation.
- More independent mentions = more evidence that the brand exists and is worth mentioning.
- Third-party sources can read as more independent than your own site — as long as it's clear who stands behind the content and it isn't covert advertising, spam, or fake reviews.

Treat the numbers from those analyses (for example, that only a minority of citations lead directly to the brand's site) as directional; results vary by platform, language, and vertical.

## Which sources show up often in AI citations

Not every site carries the same weight. These come up repeatedly in AI citations:

- **Community platforms** — Reddit and Quora (especially on [Perplexity](/blog/perplexity-seo/)), Stack Overflow for technical topics.
- **Wikipedia** — an encyclopedic, frequently cited source.
- **Reviews and comparison sites** — when they're transparent and not covert advertising.
- **Industry media and directories** — wherever your topic actually gets covered.

For narrow, vertical-specific queries, a specialized industry site, directory, or comparison platform can matter more than a global community like Reddit.

## Mentions versus links from other sites

This is where things shift from classic SEO. Links from other sites still play a role in Google rankings. For **AI citations**, though, the **brand mentions** themselves can be a strong signal — even without a link; according to published analyses from 2025–2026, that holds especially for third-party citations.

That doesn't mean ignoring links. It means adding a second goal: getting **talked about** in the places AI draws from.

## What not to do

Off-page for AI is easy to get wrong:

- **Spamming discussions and fake reviews** — communities and platform operators often catch them, and they can damage the brand.
- **Promo posts with no value** — they earn neither trust nor a citation.
- **Inconsistent positioning** — describing the brand differently in different places confuses AI too.
