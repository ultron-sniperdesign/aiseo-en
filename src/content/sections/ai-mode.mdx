## What AI Mode is and why it exists

**AI Mode is the biggest change to Google Search in a decade.** Where [AI Overviews](/answer-engine-optimization/) added an AI summary above the classic results, AI Mode leaves the classic results behind entirely: it's a separate tab where Google answers in continuous text, cites its sources, and waits for your follow-up question. Search turns into a conversation.

For users, that's convenient. For websites, it's a new deal: **either you're part of the answer, or you don't exist.** There's no tenth position for someone to scroll down to.

## How AI Mode works: query fan-out

When a user types a query ("which wax for dark paint in winter"), AI Mode **breaks it into dozens of sub-questions** — what wax is versus sealant, what winter does to paint, which products exist, what the reviews say. For each sub-question it searches the index and composes the final text with citations from the best answers.

The consequence: **the citation isn't won by the page that ranks first for the main query, but by the page that best answers a specific sub-question.** A small specialized article can beat a large authority — if it answers more precisely.

<Insight tone="aio" label="Key insight">
In AI Mode, the unit of optimization becomes **the answer to a sub-question**, not a position for a keyword. A site structured as a set of clear questions and answers has mathematically more tickets in the draw than one long text optimized for a single query.
</Insight>

## AI Mode vs. AI Overviews vs. ChatGPT

| | **AI Mode** | **AI Overviews** | **ChatGPT** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | a tab in Google | above Google's results | a separate app |
| Form | a full conversation | one summary + links | a conversation |
| Data source | Google's live index | Google's live index | training + its own search |
| User | chooses it deliberately | sees it automatically | chooses it deliberately |
| Optimization | query fan-out, citable answers | [AEO](/answer-engine-optimization/) — short answers, FAQ | [GEO](/generative-engine-optimization/) — citations, brand |

The practical message: **the same content base feeds all three.** Short answers, facts, structure, and a trustworthy brand work across the board — only the emphasis differs.

## AI Mode is arriving now, not later

Google has been rolling AI Mode out across more countries and languages, and interest in it is growing quickly. Exact feature availability keeps shifting by market — the reliable way to know where you stand is to check the search interface itself and your own Search Console data.

<Insight tone="warn" label="Watch out">
Impressions in AI Mode and AI Overviews **won't show up in Google Analytics** — they happen before the click. The only direct measure is the beta "Generative AI features" report in Search Console (impressions only for now; clicks aren't separated).
</Insight>

## What it means for your site or online store

1. **Informational queries stop bringing clicks** — the user gets the answer directly. Your content either has to sell your brand inside the answer (the citation), or target queries where an AI answer isn't enough (transactions, local services, comparisons that need personal experience).
2. **The value of being the source goes up** — when AI Mode cites your site, you gain trust and brand awareness even without a click.
3. **For e-commerce, product data decides it** — on shopping queries, AI Mode composes comparisons from specs, prices, and reviews. Structured data and solid product pages are the entry condition.
